\section{Summary}

Rare Earth Elements will continue to play a role in our society for the foreseeable future. While the supplies won't run out in our lifetime, the demand for them will continue to increase and it is important to find a way to make their use more circular. Working towards this would also help to reduce the environmental impact of mining and processing these elements, as well as to increase the security of supply. While current recycling methods have a high yield, it is important to continue the research into better recycling methods, that have the same amount of high yield but a smaller environmental footprint. By capitalising on alternative sources sich as urban mining and coal byproducts, it is also possible to clean up the environment while extracting these critical elements.
