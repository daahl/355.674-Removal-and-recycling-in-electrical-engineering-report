\begin{abstract}
    Rare earth elements (REEs) are critical components in modern technology. They play a role in everything from decarbonization to medical devices. This report examines what REEs are, where they are used, and how they are recycled. The focus is on the environmental effects of acquisition through mining, which is mostly related to land disturbance and pollution, as well as the different recycling processes, such as hydro and pyrometallurgy. The geopolitical aspects of REEs, which China is the largest supplier of, are also discussed. Lastly, there will be a look at the remaining supply of REEs and some alternative sources.
